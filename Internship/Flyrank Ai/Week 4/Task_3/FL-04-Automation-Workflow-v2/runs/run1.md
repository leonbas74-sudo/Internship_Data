### Step 1 – Gather

**Tool:** NotebookLM

**Prompt Used:**
Read the uploaded document carefully.

Create a structured summary that includes:

* Main concepts
* Important definitions

**Output:**
NotebookLM generated a structured summary covering Python fundamentals, OOP, data structures, examples, and exam-focused topics.
